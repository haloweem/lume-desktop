Lume includes only the original clock and audio visualizer, plus their required
shared variables, English date substitutions, Allura font, and Futurist
Fixed-width font. Dock, weather, player, greeting, filler, wallpaper, launcher,
and position-helper resources are intentionally excluded.

Mouse-wheel scaling is removed. Lume keeps the original base scale through the
`LumeScale` variable and manages both windows as one component-library item.
